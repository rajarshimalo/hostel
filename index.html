FIELD GUIDE

The Darjeeling
insiders know.

Not the tourist trail. The real one.

01

Nathmull's Tea Room

Third generation tea blenders. Buy first flush directly from them. Skip the market markup entirely.

12 MIN WALK
02

Tiger Hill at 4 AM

Yes, 4 AM. The Kanchenjunga sunrise is worth every cold, bleary-eyed, stumbling step up. Hire a Maruti, go.

45 MIN TAXI
03

Dekeva's Momo Corner

No sign outside. You'll smell it first. Best jhol momo in Darjeeling. Cash only. Worth every rupee.

7 MIN WALK
04

Ghoom Monastery

Oldest Tibetan Buddhist monastery in the region. Quiet, early morning visits before the tour buses. Monks in saffron. Mountains behind them.

25 MIN TAXI
05

Chowrasta Promenade

Colonial-era mall with no cars. Street food, old men playing chess, ponies, and the entire Darjeeling sky.

10 MIN WALK
06

Toy Train Platform

UNESCO-listed Darjeeling Himalayan Railway. Even if you don't ride it, stand at the platform at dusk. It's cinema.

5 MIN WALK
